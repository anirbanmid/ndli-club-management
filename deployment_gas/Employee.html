⚡ Registration Renewal Approval

Validity Active

Renewal Protocol: Every club receives 1-year registration validity upon approval. On expiration of validity, the club contacts the employee for renewal before the due renewal date. The employee verifies that required conditions are fulfilled by the club and clicks "Renewal Approved". Instantly, the current date and time is logged in the database as "Last Renewal Date", and the upcoming renewal date is extended by +1 year.

Date of Approval - Inception timestamp
Last Renewal Date None (Initial) Logged upon Renewal Approved
Upcoming Renewal Due Date - +1 Year from Last Renewal / Approval
