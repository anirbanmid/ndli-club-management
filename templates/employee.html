📥 Bulk Add New Clubs (CSV)

This door records NEW clubs only (counted as new work on the dashboard). The date columns are optional: leave them empty and dates are stamped automatically, or enter the real approval date when it is today or later (accepted formats: YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY/MM/DD, DD.MM.YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, DD-MM-YYYY). Rows carrying historical / past dates are refused here: pre-system clubs must be imported by the admin through the Data Migration portal (Bulk Club History Upload), which records them as history (counters unchanged). Nothing is written until you press the import button after a dry run. Optional admin-only column: approved_by_emp_id attributes a club to another employee — leave it empty to keep the attribution with the importing employee.

⚡ Registration Renewal Approval

Validity Active

Renewal Protocol: Every club receives 1-year registration validity upon approval. On expiration of validity, the club contacts the employee for renewal before the due renewal date. The employee verifies that required conditions are fulfilled by the club and clicks "Renewal Approved". Instantly, the current date and time is logged in the database as "Last Renewal Date", and the upcoming renewal date is extended by +1 year.

Date of Approval - Inception timestamp
Last Renewal Date None (Initial) Logged upon Renewal Approved
Upcoming Renewal Due Date - +1 Year from Last Renewal / Approval
